PROPOSED VENTURE · STUDENT CLASS CONCEPT

A small start. A longer financial journey.

Nest Egg would connect practical lessons to a paycheck plan, build a habit through check-ins, and offer an optional path to SoFi for adults 18+.

  1. LearnOne useful money concept
  2. PlanA next step you choose
  3. Build a habitRecord and review
  4. Explore SoFi · 18+Optional future path

SoFi Coach already includes budgeting and goal-planning tools. Our proposed addition is a structured lesson-to-plan journey and school or campus delivery. Whether that creates sustained use is a hypothesis to test.

About SoFi Coach (opens in a new tab)

No partnership, endorsement, account connection, or plan transfer is in place. Pilot measures would include lesson-to-plan completion, savings check-ins, and 30-day return visits.

    About Nest Egg
    Built around learning, not moving money.

    Nest Egg is a Principles of Management class concept for financial education and planning, with a proposed launch audience of ages 13–25. The pitch explores a potential SoFi venture. This demo is not a real financial service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SoFi.

    All figures and Alex’s story are illustrative. The original egg-in-nest mark is a student-project brand. The proposed teen learning experience is free; any future adult product path would be optional and for ages 18+.
