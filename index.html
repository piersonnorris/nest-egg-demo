About Nest Egg
Built around learning, not moving money.

Nest Egg is a Principles of Management class concept for financial education and planning, with a proposed launch audience of ages 13–25. The pitch explores a potential SoFi venture. This demo is not a real financial service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SoFi.

All figures and Alex’s story are illustrative. The original egg-in-nest mark is a student-project brand. The proposed teen learning experience is free; any future adult product path would be optional and for ages 18+.

This site has no account sign-in, bank APIs, analytics, or persistent storage. The optional YouTube player contacts YouTube only when loaded.
